Different relationships. Different responsibilities.

A board briefing

A focused examination of a pending technology or AI question, with alternatives, uncertainty and questions for management. Education or a briefing is not a director appointment.

Strategic advisory

A defined remit with agreed scope, cadence, access and responsibility. An advisory relationship does not itself confer board authority or an independent-director designation.

A director-role discussion

A conversation about the organization’s needs, the nature of the appointment, contribution expected, time commitment and conflicts. Suitability and independence must be assessed for the actual role.

My recorded board experience is the historical accesa.eu Executive Board role, September 2018 to July 2020. I do not present it as a current independent or non-executive directorship. The doctorate is still in progress. Dates and sources are in the professional record.

Examine the perspective before the conversation.

The Operating Advantage lens connects competitive differentiation to the capabilities needed to realize it. The research record shows the questions, methods and limits behind my AI work. Neither is a substitute for client references or an appointment-specific assessment; references can be discussed subject to permission and relevance.

Commercial interests and role boundaries.

I am a founding partner of N13 Ventures, which backs KlusAI. Any discussion involving supplier choice needs that relationship made explicit. A board or advisory remit is not conditional on buying KlusAI services.

Any related delivery proposal would need separate consideration under the organization’s conflict and procurement processes. Existing employer responsibilities, relevant interests, time commitment and any required approvals must be resolved before an engagement is accepted.

A useful first message

What does the organization need?

Include the organization and its stage, the reason for the role or briefing, the expected contribution, appointment type, time commitment and timing. A short non-confidential outline is enough. Please do not send board papers or sensitive company information in the first message.
