Technology decisions belong in the business discussion: how the company competes, where capital and attention go, what management can deliver, and what evidence allows the board to challenge the plan.

I bring managing-director, CTO, operations and historical executive-board experience, alongside engineering and ongoing AI research. For a chair, founder or investor, the starting question is which judgment the organization needs, and whether my experience and existing responsibilities fit that remit.
