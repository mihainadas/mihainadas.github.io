I am an engineer and PhD researcher at Babeș-Bolyai University. My current work asks a practical question: how far can small, openly documented language-model systems go when the data, evaluation, and infrastructure are designed together?

The research has three connected parts. TF1 generates controlled English fables from structured specifications. TF2 studies English–Romanian literary translation with open models. TF3 trains compact Romanian models from scratch and measures what they learn from synthetic narrative data. A parallel line of work examines Romanian diacritic restoration and the use of open-weight models as evaluators.

Before returning to academia, I spent more than a decade building software, leading teams, and running production projects. That background shapes how I work: define interfaces early, keep provenance, automate checks, write down failure modes, and treat reproducibility as an engineering property rather than a sentence in a paper.

This site contains the decisions and experiments that do not fit cleanly into papers. Where a post reconstructs older work, it is marked as a retrospective. Where results are preliminary, it says so.
