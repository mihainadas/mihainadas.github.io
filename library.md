Different questions need different reading. A book can supply a model for thinking. A paper can make a narrow, testable claim. A notebook can show whether an implementation does what the explanation says.

These collections connect that reading to the work on this site. Start with the form that fits your question; each collection explains its sources and limits.

## Books

[A small shelf for consequential decisions]({{ '/books/' | relative_url }}): systems, technical leadership, open-source maintenance, and machine learning. Reading selections are kept distinct from full reviews.

## Applied Papers

[Research with an operating question]({{ '/papers/' | relative_url }}). A map of 88 paper explanations, grouped by the business decision or engineering control they could inform. Published essays return to the original papers.

## Notebooks

[Executable companions]({{ '/lab-notebooks/' | relative_url }}) for checking assumptions, reproducing a calculation, and inspecting the difference between a reported result and a runnable example.
